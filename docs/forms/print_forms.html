Observer field card — laminate this page

Daily, 07:00

  1. Read rain gauge. Write mm. Empty the bottle.
  2. If pond has water: read staff gauge to 1 cm. Photograph the numbers.
  3. If water is over the crest mark, Overflow = Y.
  4. If the pond is being pumped, Pumped = Y (those days are not used for MDWIR).
  5. While the pond has water: measure W-N wells and sarchah with tape + float.

Weekly

  1. Measure all wells on the list, including control wells on the other fan.
  2. At karez outlet: time a 10 or 20 L jerrycan. Q (L/s) = litres ÷ seconds.
  3. If outlet is dry, write 0. Do not enter the tunnel.

If this dam only holds water for hours (flood-control)

  1. Do not wait for a daily 07:00 pond. Fill Form E for each flood.
  2. When safe after the peak: read the gauge, photo, mark the high-water stain.
  3. Write when overflow stopped and when the wadi went dry (hours wet).
  4. Measure wells and karez the next day, then day 3 and day 7, including the control wadi.

Rules

  • Same paint mark on the well rim every time.
  • Never go to the gauge during a flood peak.
  • If you miss a day, leave the row blank. Do not guess.
  • Give the notebook to the technician each month.
