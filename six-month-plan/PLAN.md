# Six-Month Consistency Plan (1 Oct 2026 - 4 Apr 2027)

Personalised for: 74 kg, 174 cm (BMI 24.4), small wrists and hands, fat concentrated on the belly and chest, no injuries, gym with a trainer from ~18:00 on weekdays, work 08:00-17:00, skill focus GIS and remote sensing.

Companion file: `tracker.xlsx` (26-week tracker, regenerate with `python3 build_tracker.py`).

This is a planning framework, not medical advice. Tell your trainer about the programme below and let them set exact loads; if you have any medical condition, check with a doctor before starting the calorie deficit.

---

## 1. The diagnosis and what it changes

Your weight and BMI are normal, but the fat sits centrally and your frame is small. That pattern responds best to body recomposition, not dieting alone:

- A mild calorie deficit (about 300-400 kcal/day), never a crash diet. Crash dieting on a small frame loses muscle and leaves the belly.
- High protein (130-150 g/day) so the deficit takes fat, not muscle.
- Progressive strength training 3-4 times per week. New lifters can gain muscle and lose fat at the same time for most of the first six months.
- Daily steps (8,000 rising to 10,000) and 7-8 h sleep. Short sleep raises appetite and preferentially stores fat around the waist.
- Measure the waist, not just the scale. With recomposition the scale moves slowly while the waist drops.

Small wrists are skeletal and do not limit muscle growth, but grip and wrist stability will limit your lifts early. The programme handles this with neutral-grip dumbbells for the first four weeks, wrist warm-ups, two grip finishers per week, and lifting straps from week 8 so grip never caps leg and back progress. Belly and chest fat cannot be spot-reduced; they come off through the overall deficit. Chest appearance also improves with pressing volume and posture work (rows, face pulls, thoracic mobility), because desk posture rounds the upper back and pushes the chest forward.

## 5. Training programme (proposal for your trainer)

Give this section to your trainer. They choose exact exercises and loads; the structure, progression and grip strategy are what matter.

### Cycle 1 (Weeks 1-13): three full-body sessions, alternating A/B

Mon/Wed/Fri: Week 1 = A, B, A; Week 2 = B, A, B; and so on. Each session starts with 8 minutes of warm-up: 5 minutes bike or rower, wrist circles and wrist flexion/extension with a light dumbbell, band pull-aparts, 10 bodyweight squats.

Session A

| Exercise | Weeks 1-4 | Weeks 5-13 | Sets x reps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Squat pattern | Goblet squat | Barbell back squat (or leg press if the trainer prefers) | 3 x 8-12 |
| Horizontal push | Dumbbell bench press, neutral grip | Barbell bench press from week 9 if wrists are comfortable, stacked wrists | 3 x 8-12 |
| Hip hinge | Dumbbell Romanian deadlift | Barbell RDL or trap-bar deadlift, straps allowed from week 8 | 3 x 8-12 |
| Horizontal pull | Chest-supported dumbbell row | Seated cable row | 3 x 10-12 |
| Core | Plank | Long-lever or weighted plank | 3 x 30-60 s |
| Grip finisher | Farmer carry | Farmer carry, heavier | 3 x 30-40 m |

Session B

| Exercise | Weeks 1-4 | Weeks 5-13 | Sets x reps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-leg | Split squat or walking lunge | Bulgarian split squat | 3 x 8-10 per leg |
| Vertical pull | Lat pulldown | Assisted chin-up, aiming at the first strict rep by week 13 | 3 x 8-12 |
| Vertical push | Seated dumbbell shoulder press, neutral grip | Same, heavier | 3 x 8-12 |
| Glute/hamstring | Glute bridge | Hip thrust or 45-degree back extension | 3 x 10-12 |
| Upper back and posture | Face pull | Face pull or rear-delt fly | 3 x 12-15 |
| Core | Side plank | Pallof press | 3 x 30 s / 10 reps |
| Grip finisher | Dead hang | Dead hang, target 60 s by week 13 | 3 x max time |

Loading rules

- Weeks 1-2: 2 sets per exercise, RPE 5-6 (4-5 reps left in the tank). Purpose: technique and habit.
- Weeks 3-8: 3 sets, RPE 7-8. Double progression: when you reach the top of the rep range on all sets, add 2.5 kg (upper body) or 5 kg (lower body) and start again at the bottom of the range.
- Week 9: deload. 2 sets, 40% less load, same movements.
- Weeks 10-12: 3-4 sets, main lifts 6-10 reps at RPE 8, accessories 10-15.
- Week 13: benchmark week. Mon: back squat 5RM, bench press 5RM, push-ups to failure. Wed: deadlift 5RM, strict chin-ups, dead hang, plank. Fri: light full body plus 12-minute Cooper test. Sun: measurements, photos, Cycle 1 review.

Cardio and steps

- Tue/Thu: Zone 2 cardio (you can speak full sentences, roughly 60-70% of max heart rate). 30 min in weeks 1-4, 35-40 min from week 5. Incline treadmill walk, bike, rower or elliptical.
- Sat: 45-60 min of something you enjoy.
- Sun: 20 min mobility.
- Steps: 8,000/day in weeks 0-4, 10,000/day from week 5 (the two 15-minute walks plus normal movement get you there).

Wrist and grip strategy

- Neutral-grip dumbbells for pressing in weeks 1-4; move to barbells only when wrists feel stable, with knuckles stacked over the forearm.
- Wrist warm-up every session; wrist wraps only for heavy pressing if there is discomfort.
- Two grip finishers per week (farmer carry, dead hang) build the forearms, which is what changes the look of a small wrist.
- Lifting straps from week 8 on RDL, deadlift and heavy rows so grip fatigue never limits leg and back development. Grip is trained separately by the finishers.

### Cycle 2 (Weeks 14-26): four-day upper/lower split

Mon Lower A, Tue Upper A, Wed Zone 2 40 min, Thu Lower B, Fri Upper B, Sat intervals (8 x 1 min hard / 2 min easy on bike or rower, or a run) or sport, Sun mobility.

| Session | Exercises (sets x reps) |
|---|---|
| Lower A | Back squat 4 x 5-8, Romanian deadlift 3 x 8-10, leg press or split squat 3 x 10-12, calf raise 3 x 12-15, hanging knee raise 3 x 10 |
| Upper A | Bench press 4 x 5-8, chin-up or lat pulldown 4 x 6-10, dumbbell shoulder press 3 x 8-12, cable row 3 x 10-12, face pull 3 x 15, farmer carry 3 x 40 m |
| Lower B | Trap-bar or conventional deadlift 4 x 5 (straps allowed), front squat or hack squat 3 x 8-10, hip thrust 3 x 10-12, leg curl 3 x 12, plank 3 x 60 s |
| Upper B | Incline dumbbell press 4 x 8-10, pull-up or pulldown 4 x 8-10, dips or close-grip push-up 3 x 8-12, one-arm dumbbell row 3 x 10-12, lateral raise 3 x 12-15, dead hang 3 x max |

Weeks 14-21: add load with the same double-progression rule. Week 22: deload. Weeks 23-25: heaviest blocks, main lifts 4 x 5-6. Week 26: final benchmarks (same tests as week 13), measurements, photos, review.

### Benchmarks (record in the Benchmarks sheet)

Day 1-4, then every 4 weeks for measurements, and the full test battery in weeks 13 and 26: weight, waist at navel, chest, hips, resting heart rate, push-ups to failure, dead hang, plank, strict chin-ups, goblet squat 10RM (Day 1 only), back squat 5RM, deadlift 5RM, bench press 5RM, 12-minute Cooper distance, average daily steps, average sleep, photos (front, side, back, same light and clothing).

### Rules that make it automatic

- Protein first on every plate, vegetables at lunch and dinner, oil measured with a spoon.
- Eat out at most twice a week, and order protein plus vegetables first.

- Adjust every 2 weeks, not every day: if weight and waist have not moved in 14 days, remove 150 kcal (the dinner starch) or add 1,500 steps. Change one thing at a time.
- Week 13 checkpoint: waist below 87 cm means move to maintenance (about 2,400 kcal) and train for muscle; otherwise continue the deficit.
- Week 22: 7-day diet break at maintenance, deliberately, together with the training deload.

## 7. Skill block: GIS and remote sensing (130 hours over 26 weeks)

Rules: 60 minutes Mon-Fri, timer on, phone in another room. Optional 90-minute project session on Saturday. One commit pushed to a public portfolio repository every Friday. Every module ends with a deliverable you can show.

Pick one study area on Day 1 (a province or city you know) and use it for every module; by week 26 you will have a complete case study.

### Week 0 (1-4 Oct): setup

Install Miniforge or conda and create an environment with geopandas, rasterio, rioxarray, xarray, rasterstats, matplotlib, jupyterlab, scikit-learn, earthengine-api and geemap. Install VS Code and Git. Create the public portfolio repository. Register for Google Earth Engine now (non-commercial Cloud project), because approval can take days. Download baseline data for the study area (admin boundaries, roads, a DEM).
