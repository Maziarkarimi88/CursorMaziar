REAL / OPEN DATA SOURCES TO USE ALONGSIDE SYNTHETIC LAB DATA
==============================================================
1) Administrative boundaries (real):
   - Humanitarian Data Exchange (HDX) Afghanistan COD-AB or your country COD
   - https://data.humdata.org/

2) Population (real):
   - WorldPop population grids (100 m / 1 km)
   - https://www.worldpop.org/
   - Meta/Data for Good High Resolution Population Density Maps

3) Points of interest / buildings (real):
   - OpenStreetMap extracts via Geofabrik
   - https://download.geofabrik.de/

4) Optional telecom open references (conceptual / regulatory):
   - National telecom regulatory open datasets (site registers where published)
   - OpenCelliD (community cell tower DB) — https://opencellid.org/
     NOTE: Use only for training illustration; validate license/attribution.

5) Basemaps:
   - Esri World Topographic / Imagery (ArcGIS Pro)
   - OpenStreetMap basemap

In this Day 3 package, MarketingZones + Subscribers are SYNTHETIC (MetroTel Capital Region)
so the class can run without sensitive customer data. Replace with anonymized real
subscriber extracts when available (never use live PII in training).
